DELVE · ART LAB

Tuning sandbox for the cave look. Every panel here renders through the exact same shared modules the game uses — cave-render.js (rock), ore-art.js (ore/gem veins), sprites.js (miner) and lighting.js (the geometry-aware lamp/fog/vignette) — so what you see here is what ships. Iterate a module and both the lab and the game move together.
